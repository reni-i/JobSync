# JobSync — Smart Job Search

JobSync is a modern job search landing page designed to help job seekers discover opportunities based on their real skills and transferable experience.

Instead of endlessly scrolling through job listings, JobSync focuses on helping users understand their existing skills and find roles that better match their experience.

## 🌐 Live Demo

https://reni-i.github.io/JobSync/

## 📌 Project Overview

Finding a suitable job can be overwhelming, especially when job seekers are unsure whether their existing experience matches a particular position.

JobSync presents a smarter approach to job searching by focusing on skills rather than relying only on previous job titles.

The website introduces an AI-powered job matching concept where users can upload their resume, understand their skills, and discover job opportunities that are relevant to their experience.

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Help users discover jobs based on their actual skills.
- Highlight transferable skills from previous experiences.
- Make the job-search process feel simpler and less overwhelming.
- Present job matching information in a clear and user-friendly interface.

## ✨ Features

### 🔍 Skill-Based Matching
Matches job opportunities based on the user's skills and transferable experience.

### 📊 Job Match Score
Provides a visual match score to help users quickly understand how suitable a job is for their profile.

### 📄 Resume Upload
Allows users to upload their resume as the starting point for understanding their skills.

### 💡 Skill Insights
Helps users recognize skills they already have and how those skills can apply to different roles.

### 💼 Better Job Discovery
Encourages users to explore roles beyond their previous job titles.

### 📱 Responsive Design
Designed to provide a consistent experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
